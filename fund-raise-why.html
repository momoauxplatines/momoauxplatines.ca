goals are productive

Setting yourself a goal limits the time spent doubting. And since time is precious, we should spend most of it doing good things.

people need a hand

I needed a hand this past year. I was lucky to meet the right people at the right time, and to benefit instantly from my family's, friends' and community's support. But not everybody has the same chance, at the right moment or at all. So let's be there for them.

science helps a lot

To battle cancer, science shows up. It did for me and for all of us battling this beast every day — helping to spot it fast and precisely, to get the cancer out efficiently, and to minimise the aftershock so people can go back to their life in a flash.

care and inspire

Just like the Quebec Cancer Foundation, I'd like you to join the fundraiser to give back to the people who need extra help while facing this challenging situation — providing someone to talk to, shelter, therapy and many other game-changing services to cancer fighters across Quebec.
