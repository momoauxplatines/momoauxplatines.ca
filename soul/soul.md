# soul  
  
## What is DJing?  
Being a DJ is all about sharing. A its base, a DJ is a music lover who listens to a lot of music. Then groups songs into genres. Then into moods. And finally links songs together in creative ways.  
  
## Why Momo as a DJ?  
Momo is a curious music lover whose ears are always active. Curating songs into selective ambiances meant to decorate a certain venue or event’s airwaves. Mixing songs like if they knew each other. Like if they were used to hold hands or hug. Adding an additional touch of turntablism, sampling and letting the ambiance breathe a little to be able to read the room and react according to it signs.  
  
## Who is Momo DJing for?  
Momo wants to create a bond with humans. Talk a little while the sample runs. Make eye contact to engage in conversation. Please people with song requests and surround them with new discoveries. Momo likes to DJ in the crowd. Behind the bar. In shared places where interactions can manifest themselves.  
  
## Momo as a teacher  
Momo wants to share the art of being a DJ. How to build playlists. Different ways to mix songs depending of the context in which you are set. How to pace the DJ set. Build an ambiance. How to work different tech and materials. Know what DJs did in the past and what they are doing in the present. Creating the path in which the new DJ can explore and travel through music.
